# Corrected scientific-question amendment

## Primary question

Which leakage-safe combination of registered temporal view, actor-centred RGB
appearance, structured spatio-temporal feature groups, imbalance treatment,
sequence encoder, fusion configuration, and auxiliary supervision gives the
strongest recognition of the ten behaviors on the pooled reviewed population,
with native-unit Macro-F1 as the primary outcome and explicit protection for
fight, social-nose, and other frozen weak or rare classes?

The ten-class order is:

`drink, eat, fight, social-nose, explore, lying, stand, move, sitting,
playwithtoy`.

## Bounded secondary questions

1. Which of `T6`, `T8`, `T12`, and `T16` is best under a fixed sequential
   reference configuration?
2. Which of RGB, geometry, motion, ROI, social, and full canonical 46D input
   families adds evidence under matched controls?
3. Which small registered imbalance treatment improves rare-class recognition
   without common-class collapse or unacceptable calibration harm?
4. Do retained ROI, causal-history, social, fusion, or masked posture modules
   survive matched multi-fold, multi-seed screening?
5. Does the frozen selected procedure reproduce under untouched grouped outer
   folds with complete native-unit coverage?

## Explicit non-questions

This campaign does not select a legacy-versus-additional-source winner, prove
that the collections are identical domains, require both provenances in every
outer test fold, select a source-balanced subset as the scientific population,
or change grouped folds after observing metrics. Source provenance remains an
audit and diagnostic-slicing field and is excluded from predictive input.

`Hidden` remains a binary frame-object visibility decision (`Yes`/`No`), not a
behavior-uncertainty value. Review metadata, paths, IDs, partition fields,
source identifiers, and target-derived fields remain outside model X.

Only the frozen outer OOF package may support headline behavior-performance
claims. E0, inner screening, and bounded autoresearch remain engineering or
model-selection evidence.
